# FW Lab Overview

### What is Firmware (FW)?

Firmware is the intersection of software (code) and hardware (circuitry). 

Basically, FW is how we're able to talk (through code) to our printed circuit boards (circuitry) and logically control the functions they are capable of through their circuit design. 

We believe that good hardware engineers are dependent on knowing how the firmware is written, and all good embedded programmers know how hardware is made!

### What will I learn in this Lab?

In this lab, you will learn about...
- The **coding language** (C/C++) that communicates desired logic to circuitry, and how **embedded firmware** is written.
- The intermediary hardware (what bridges the software and the circuitry), AKA: the **microcontroller.** The code goes in this, and the microcontroller communicates it to the circuit through signals.
- **Design fundamentals** applicable to any programming/embedded project (e.g. FSMs, functional programming, modularity).
- Types of communication, such as **UART, I2C, CAN, and SPI**, along with other tools and implementations. These have different capabilities ideal for different hardware (i.e. OLED screen displays vs. brake pedals!)

### How is this Lab Structured?

This lab will give you each of these concepts in bite-sized readings, and most will have corresponding exercises so that you can practice your newfound knowledge! There will then be a final project for you to test your knowledge!
