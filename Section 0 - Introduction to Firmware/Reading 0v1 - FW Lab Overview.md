# FW Lab Overview

### What is Firmware (FW)?

Firmware is the intersection of software (code) and hardware (circuitry). 

Basically, FW is how we're able to talk (through code) to our printed circuit boards (circuitry) and tell it how to logically control the functions it is capable of through its circuit design.
<br><br>

### How will I learn that in this Lab?

In this lab, you will learn about...
- The **coding language** (C/C++) that communicates desired logic to circuitry.
- The intermediary hardware (what bridges the software and the circuitry), AKA: the **microcontroller.** The code goes in this, and the microcontroller communicates it to the circuit through signals.
- Types of communication, such as **UART, I2C, CAN, and SPI.** These have different capabilities ideal for different hardware (i.e. OLED screen displays vs. brake pedals!)

<br><br>
### How is this Lab Structured?

This lab will give you each of these concepts in bite-sized readings, and most will have corresponding exercises so that you can practice your newfound knowledge!
