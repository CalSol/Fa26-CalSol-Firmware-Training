## What is Complexity and how can we deal with it?

Well, it's anything that makes it hard to understand and write your program. But, what does this look like?

### Dependencies and Information Overload

Let's say I'm trying to create code that turns on brake lights when the brake pedal is pressed.
This begs a few questions. 
- How does the car know the brake pedal is being pressed?
- How does the car know how to time keeping the lights on?
- How does it keep time?
- How do the pedals on the bottom shell talk to the brake lights on the top shell?
- How do the brake lights vary in intensity?

There are a lot of questions that need to be answered and to be honest, it seems intimidating. To even understand
one simple part of the car, you need to understand a bunch of other things.

Another example (which some of you may recognize and others will see in the future) is the RISC-V CPU.
