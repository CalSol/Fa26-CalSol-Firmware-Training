## Welcome to CalSol Firmware Training!
This lab is meant to teach you how to think about firmware while giving enough practice to get you started writing it.

### Foreword
The firmware lab will seem challenging, but succeeding in everything is not the point. The goal will be to walk out of the lab being able to conceptually
understand firmware, while getting some reps in writing it. We are looking for effort and understanding, not code accuracy, so please use
this as an opportunity to learn.

### Disclaimer on Section 6
Section 6 additional reading is more challenging and not fully needed to complete the final project. We urge ambitious members to try and understand the sections,
but we would prefer for members to prioritize the other sections more heavily. However, the bonus tasks of the project can dip into the content of the additional
readings, so use this section as a resource.
